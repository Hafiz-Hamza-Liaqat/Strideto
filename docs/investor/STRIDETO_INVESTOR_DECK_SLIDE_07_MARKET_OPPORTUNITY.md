## Speaker notes

Rather than relying on a broad industry-market headline, STRIDETO uses a bottom-up model built from employer populations in validated priority markets and our own annual revenue per paying organization. The base model produces a $424.4 million TAM and $383.5 million SAM. Our three-year SOM remains deliberately conservative at 531 paying organizations, representing approximately $21.3K in annualized revenue at the base ARPA. Education, mobility and professional-service providers provide additional monetization upside but are excluded from headline TAM until stronger organization-level denominators are available.

## Scope guardrails

- Base case is the headline; Conservative and Upside scenarios remain sensitivity/appendix data.
- The $424.4M figure is a validated multi-market bottom-up TAM, not an exhaustive worldwide employer market claim.
- Core headline market is employer-led.
- Institutions and individual users are excluded from revenue TAM.
- All values are USD.
- Provider opportunity is upside only and is not included in TAM, SAM, or SOM.
